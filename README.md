# Bakery-Redesign

## Purpose 

Redesign the website of a small custom-order bakery to improve its content organization, usability, and presentation

## Problem

Carmen's bakery contains substantial business information, including products, pricing, customization options, events, and the owner's story. However, these are presented in a lengthy page without sufficiently distinct content sections or dedicated destinations.


## Business Objectives

-  Increase product discoverability.
-  Make custom-order inquiries easier.
-  Communicate pricing and customization options.
- Promote upcoming events.

## Intended Users

- Potential customers discovering the bakery.
- Returning customers looking for products or pricing.
- Customers interested in placing custom orders.


## Primary Goal 

Make product discovery straightforward while providing accessible paths to additional business information.

## Design Philosophy

Prioritize the bakery's products and services. Allow visitors to explore the business's identity and story as secondary information.

## Scope

### Core

-  Home Page 
- Dedicated menu/products page
- About page
-  Contact/order information

### Deferred

-  Events page
-  Product filtering
-  Interactive ordering
### Out of Scope

-  Payment processing
-  Order management system
-  Customer accounts

### Technology Stack

-  HTML
-  CSS
-  JavaScript



Independent concept redesign. Not affiliated with the referenced business.
